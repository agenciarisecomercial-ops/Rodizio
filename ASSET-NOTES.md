## Prompt usado

Use case: product-mockup. Create a photorealistic luxury sushi website animation asset on a genuinely transparent background, square 1024x1024 composition. One single thick salmon sushi roll held firmly by a pair of elegant dark wooden chopsticks extending diagonally from the right edge toward the center. The roll is centered at x=480,y=600, about 330 pixels wide; top surface around y=440, bottom around y=750. Show a beautiful slightly top-down three-quarter view: glossy orange salmon wrapped around white rice with avocado and cream cheese core, detailed rice grains. The top surface must be clearly visible and ready to receive sauce animated later. Leave upper 40% of canvas completely empty transparent for a pouring sauce animation. Chopsticks pinch the sides below the top, with both sticks visible and physically plausible, ends reach right edge. Warm studio side lighting, realistic appetizing textures, editorial macro food photography, crisp subject. No sauce stream, no dripping sauce, no plate, no hands, no text, no branding, no decoration, no background. This is an illustrative hero asset, not a specific real menu dish.
